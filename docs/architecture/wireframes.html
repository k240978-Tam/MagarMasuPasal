Design decisions

  • Cart never leaves view. No page navigation mid-sale — right rail is always live.
  • 64px+ touch targets on every product card; category chips filter instantly, no reload.
  • Weight/qty entry is a generic component — triggered by any unit-tracked product, not meat-specific.
  • Payment buttons render from the tenant's configured Payment Manager gateways — adding a gateway later adds a button, not a rebuild.
  • Shortcut bar is persistent and toggleable — cashier speed over mouse-driven flows.

Design decisions

  • Pure consumer, no input. Every pixel here is driven by TerminalStateUpdated broadcasts from the paired POS over Reverb — no polling, no local logic.
  • Commits to one dark, high-contrast look regardless of the shop's chosen theme — legible from a distance, in bright shop lighting, day or night.
  • Idle state (no active cart) rotates business announcements/promotions full-bleed instead of an empty screen.
  • State transitions animate (fade/slide) — item added → payment pending → success → thank-you → idle — never a hard refresh.

Design decisions

  • Widgets are permission-aware — a Cashier sees their own shift; an Owner sees all branches; an Accountant sees P&L instead of stock alerts.
  • Live tiles update via a debounced DashboardMetricUpdated broadcast, not a page refresh.
  • Alert severity encoded in form (dot color), not just text — scannable at a glance.
  • Widget set is config-driven per business type, never hardcoded per industry.
